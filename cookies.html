Cookie Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, such as your preferences and login information.

2. How We Use Cookies

Guidana uses cookies for various purposes to improve your experience and ensure the proper functioning of our Services:

  • Authentication: To keep you logged in to your account
  • Preferences: To remember your settings and preferences
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with our Services
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraudulent activity
  • Performance: To optimize website speed and functionality
  • Marketing: To deliver relevant content and advertisements (with consent)

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Duration Purpose
Essential Cookies Session Required for website functionality, security, and authentication
Analytics Cookies Up to 2 years Help us understand user behavior and improve Services
Preference Cookies Up to 1 year Store user preferences and settings
Marketing Cookies Up to 2 years Used for targeted advertising (only with consent)

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may allow third-party service providers to place cookies on your device for analytics, advertising, and other purposes. These third parties include Google Analytics, social media platforms, advertising partners, and payment processors.

5. Cookie Control

5.1 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can accept all cookies, reject all cookies, accept only essential cookies, or delete cookies from your device.

5.2 Browser Do-Not-Track

If your browser has a "Do-Not-Track" feature, we respect this signal and will not track your activity across third-party websites.

6. GDPR and Privacy Law Compliance

For users in the European Union and other regions with data protection laws, we obtain explicit consent before using non-essential cookies.
